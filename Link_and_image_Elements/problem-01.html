GLA University


About

GLA University is a private university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, India. It was established in 1998 and offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in various fields such as engineering, management, law, pharmacy, and more. The university is known for its emphasis on research, innovation, and holistic development of students.

Programs

GLA University offers a diverse range of programs across multiple disciplines. Some of the popular programs include:

  • Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in various engineering streams
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA)
  • Bachelor of Science (B.Sc) in various fields
  • Master of Science (M.Sc) in specialized areas
  • Doctoral programs (Ph.D.) in research-oriented fields

Campus Life

GLA University provides a vibrant campus life with various extracurricular activities, clubs, and events. Students have access to modern facilities, including libraries, laboratories, sports complexes, and hostels. The university encourages students to participate in cultural festivals, technical competitions, and community service initiatives.

Admissions

Admissions to GLA University are based on merit and performance in the respective entrance examinations. The university also considers other factors such as work experience, leadership qualities, and extracurricular achievements during the selection process.
