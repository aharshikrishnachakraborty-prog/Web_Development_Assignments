About us

Saffron & Sage is a contemporary fine-dining restaurant located in the heart of Sadar, Uttar Pradesh, India. Founded in 2024 by culinary visionary Anaya Verma, our mission is to blend traditional Indian spices with modern global flavors. We pride ourselves on sourcing locally, ensuring every dish is fresh, sustainably made, and a feast for the senses. Our ambiance reflects a fusion of rustic charm and minimalist elegance, ideal for both intimate dinners and lively gatherings

Signature Items

  • Spiced Lamb Chops: Tender lamb chops marinated in a blend of saffron, garlic, and Indian spices, grilled to perfection.
  • Truffle Paneer Tikka: A modern twist on the classic paneer tikka, infused with truffle oil and served with a tangy mint chutney.
  • Masala Risotto: Creamy risotto cooked with aromatic Indian spices, finished with a hint of saffron and Parmesan cheese.
  • Mango Lassi Panna Cotta: A delightful dessert combining the creamy texture of panna cotta with the tropical sweetness of mango lassi.
