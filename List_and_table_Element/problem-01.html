GLA University

Admission Requirements

  1. Minimum academic score- 70%
  2. Completion of prerequisite courses
  3. Submission of application form

Eligibility

Applicants must have completed high school or equivalent
Applicants must have a valid diploma or certificate from an accredited institution.
Applicants must meet the minimum academic score requirement
Applicants must have a minimum academic score of 70% in their previous qualifying examination.
Applicants must provide proof of prerequisite courses
Applicants must submit transcripts or certificates demonstrating completion of required prerequisite courses.

Application Process

  1. Fill out the online application form on the university website.
  2. Submit all required documents, including academic transcripts and proof of prerequisite courses.
  3. Pay the application fee as specified on the website.
  4. Attend an interview or entrance examination if required by the program.
  5. Wait for the admission decision and notification from the university.

    6. Required Documents

    • Academic transcripts from previous educational institutions
    • Proof of completion of prerequisite courses
    • Valid identification documents (passport, national ID, etc.)
    • Letters of recommendation (if required)
    • Personal statement or essay (if required)

    Important Terms

    Application Fee
    The non-refundable fee that applicants must pay when submitting their application.
    Prerequisite Courses
    Specific courses that applicants must have completed before applying to the program.
    Admission Decision
    The final decision made by the university regarding an applicant's acceptance or rejection.

    Fee Structure

    • Tuition Fees: $10,000 per academic year
    • Accommodation Fees: $3,000 per academic year
    • Library and Laboratory Fees: $500 per academic year
    • Miscellaneous Fees: $1,000 per academic year
