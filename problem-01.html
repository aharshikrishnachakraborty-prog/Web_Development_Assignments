GLA University Greater Noida

About my college

GLA University is a private university in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. It is recognized by University Grants Commission (UGC), National Council for Teacher Education and Pharmacy Council of India.
It has been accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with 'A+' Grade.

Courses Offered <

GLA University - Greater Noida Campus offers UG and PG level courses. Students wanting to take admission can register themselves in different courses as per eligibility. Some of the offered programs are B.E. / B.Tech, BBA, BCA, etc.
Moreover, GLA University - Greater Noida Campus offers various programs in different specialisations as well
