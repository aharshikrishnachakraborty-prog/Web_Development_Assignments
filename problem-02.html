Education

GLA University is a private University and located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The university is recognised by the UGC also accredited by the NAAC A+ Grade and IACBE. GLA University is know for its popular programmes are BTech, BBA, MBA and MCA. Some of the key highlights of GLA University Greater Noida are given below: Fee Structure: The total course fee for GLA University BTech fee ranges between INR 9.48 lakh- INR 10.88 lakh. Admission Dates: GLA University admission is based on entrance exams. The university conducts GLAET entrance exams and followed by the personal interview. Placements: The average package offered at GLA University Greater Noida placements 2025 was INR 6 LPA- INR 8 LPA. Amuni Network: The university has 51,000+ Global Alumni Network. International Alumni: The university has 6,000+ professional working abroad.
