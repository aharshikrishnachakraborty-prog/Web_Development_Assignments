Tejashwi dares Chirag Paswan, Manjhi to contest from unreserved seats


What Happened?

Tejashwi Singh, a prominent political figure, has issued a challenge to Chirag Paswan and Manjhi, urging them to contest the upcoming elections from unreserved seats.

Where Did It Happen?

The announcement was made during a public meeting in Lucknow, where Tejashwi Singh addressed a crowd of supporters and political enthusiasts.

When

The challenge was issued earlier today, during the morning hours.

Why

Tejashwi Singh stated that the move is aimed at promoting inclusivity and giving voice to underrepresented communities in the political arena.

Who Was Involved?

The announcement involved Tejashwi Singh, Chirag Paswan, and Manjhi, along with their respective teams and supporters.
