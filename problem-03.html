Nepal Floods and Disaster Response A severe flash flood in Nepal’s Rasuwa district triggered by the Bhote Koshi River has caused widespread destruction, destroying homes, bridges, and infrastructure.
Reports confirm at least seventeen deaths and over one hundred Indian nationals missing, many of whom were pilgrims traveling to the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra. Helicopters, drones, and ground rescue teams are actively searching for survivors and stranded individuals.
The flood is believed to have been worsened by an ice-rock avalanche, and authorities are investigating a potential link to earthquake activity. Videos circulating on social media vividly depict the destructive power of the floodwaters, leaving communities in shock. The Prime Ministers of India and Nepal, including PM Narendra Modi and PM Balen Shah, have voiced solidarity and mobilized emergency aid efforts for relief operations Indiatimes.com . India-Specific Updates Several states, particularly Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, are on high alert due to potential spillover effects of the flood.
